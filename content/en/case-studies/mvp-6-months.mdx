goal_metrics: Ship MVP in six months, validate product-market signal, and establish a scalable roadmap baseline.
discovery: Defined core user journeys, legal/compliance constraints, and non-negotiable adoption blockers.
options_tradeoffs: Evaluated breadth-first launch vs. narrow but complete end-to-end flow for one core segment.
execution: Prioritized an end-to-end MVP path, coordinated cross-functional squads, and tracked weekly decision log.
results: MVP launched on time with a clear learning agenda and data-backed next-priority roadmap.
learnings: Strong scope discipline and transparent decision logs are key to 0→1 speed.

## Delivery model

Used explicit guardrails to keep scope tight while preserving product integrity.

## Key takeaway

Speed came from deliberate tradeoffs, not from cutting quality blindly.
