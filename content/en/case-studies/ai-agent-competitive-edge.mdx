options_tradeoffs: Compared generic chatbot approach vs. domain-specific task agent. Chose narrow scope first to reduce false answers.
execution: Scoped an MVP agent for high-value intents, integrated with internal knowledge and service APIs, then released in phases.

learnings: AI differentiation works when tied to clear user jobs and guarded by quality thresholds.

## Why this mattered

The team needed proof that AI was improving business outcomes, not just adding a feature.

## What we shipped

A focused AI agent path with measurable KPIs and phased rollout controls.
