context: The sales-assisted checkout experience had too many handoffs and weak transparency for procurement teams.
goal_metrics: Increase completion by 20%, reduce median time-to-purchase by 30%, and improve NPS by 10 points.
discovery: Interviewed sales, support, and 12 procurement leads; analyzed funnel events and contract review timelines.
options_tradeoffs: Compared a guided checkout wizard against inline contextual editing. The wizard reduced errors but increased click depth.
execution: Shipped a hybrid model with inline edits plus a step summary drawer, contract preview, and payment method recommendations.
results: Checkout completion improved by 27%, median purchase time decreased by 34%, and support tickets dropped by 19%.
learnings: Procurement confidence improves most when pricing rationale and legal checkpoints are visible early.

## Problem

Enterprise buyers needed confidence before committing, but key details surfaced too late in the flow.

## Approach

I partnered with product and engineering to redesign information architecture, then ran moderated usability tests before rollout.

## Outcome

The new flow improved completion and also reduced sales cycle friction for larger deals.
