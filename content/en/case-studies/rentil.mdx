role: Product Manager & Systems Analyst
context: A B2B platform for property management companies handling 50-500 properties, built to centralize operational, financial, and service workflows in one system.
goal_metrics: Create one operational platform for properties, tenants, contracts, payments, and service tasks while supporting real property-management workflows at scale.
discovery: The product need was not another CRM but a multi-entity operational system with a broad database model, role-based flows, and strong linkage between service, finance, and lease activity.
options_tradeoffs: Product decisions had to balance workflow depth, database complexity, payment integration, and delivery continuity during backend team changes.
execution: Defined product structure, specified end-to-end workflows, planned the database, shaped the platform capabilities, and kept delivery moving through backend team replacement.
results: Rentil established a product foundation for property-management companies, connecting daily operations, lease handling, payments, tasks, and records in one platform.
learnings: Strong platform design in PropTech requires systems thinking across entities, workflows, finance, and operational ownership, not just CRM screens.
