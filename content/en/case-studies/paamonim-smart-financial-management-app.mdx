role: Product Management
context: Paamonim promotes balanced and responsible financial conduct, and the app was built as a central digital tool for managing income, expenses, debts, assets, and budget in one place.
goal_metrics: Increase the app’s usability and day-to-day value, respond faster to business needs, create a continuous learning loop, improve adoption through digital tools, and modernize aging infrastructure without harming the experience.
discovery: The work relied on an active product with existing users and on conversations with end users in order to identify field needs and drive continuous improvement from real usage.
options_tradeoffs: The main tradeoff was balancing new feature work, bug fixing, and stability improvements under a very limited budget and aggressive prioritization constraints.
execution: Led new feature work, UX improvements, re-evaluation of existing features, translation of business and user needs into product decisions, and close collaboration with development, design, and QA.
results: 80% of user traffic shifted to working from within the app, return rate increased by 15%, and time spent in the app rose by about 20%.
learnings: The project reinforced the value of sharp prioritization, field-driven product thinking, and continuous improvement of an existing system without disrupting ongoing user workflows.
