role: Product Manager
context: Delivery depended on multiple external systems with unstable SLAs and changing interface contracts.
goal_metrics: Reduce onboarding delays, improve successful sync rates, and lower manual exception handling.
discovery: Audited dependency map, contract mismatch patterns, and recurring support escalations.
options_tradeoffs: Considered direct point-to-point integrations vs. adapter-based contracts with fallback queues.
execution: Implemented adapter contracts, retry/fallback flows, and proactive monitoring for critical partner endpoints.
results: Higher reliability across integrations and faster issue triage for both product and operations teams.
learnings: Contract-first APIs and fallback design are mandatory in dependency-heavy products.

## Challenge

External dependencies created uncertainty and frequent coordination overhead.

## Outcome

A structured integration model that improved resilience and predictability.
