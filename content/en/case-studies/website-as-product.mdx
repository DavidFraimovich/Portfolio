description: A case study on treating the portfolio itself as a product, with clear users, decision paths, proof layers, and sharing flows.
date: 2024-06-01
tags:
  - Product Strategy

role: Product Manager / Designer / Builder
context: PM candidate evaluation happens under time pressure and fragmented context, so the site needed to build trust quickly without creating reading fatigue.
goal_metrics: Reduce time-to-value, create clear paths for recruiters, hiring managers, and referrals, and centralize proof of impact and decision-making.
discovery: Mapped 3 core audience types, the questions they ask in the first few minutes, and the gap between a linear resume and a more persuasive product narrative.
options_tradeoffs: Considered a generic portfolio site, a richer resume-only page, and a case-study-led structure. Chose the architecture that prioritized clarity, trust signals, and depth on demand.
execution: Built a bilingual static site with clear navigation paths, structured case studies, layered content for different audiences, and direct contact entry points.
results: Created a single asset that works as a quick introduction, a deeper proof layer for product thinking, and a shareable link inside hiring loops.
learnings: A portfolio converts better when it behaves like a product, with users, pains, flows, success criteria, and content matched to the reader's available time.

## Why this matters

Instead of treating the site like a gallery of work, I treated it like a product with a clear job: help people decide quickly whether there is a strong fit.

## What this case study includes

This page brings together the product brief, user tracks, impact signals, featured work, decision log, latest posts, and a shareable summary in one place.
