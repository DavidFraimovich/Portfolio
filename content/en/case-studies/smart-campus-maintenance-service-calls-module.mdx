role: Systems Analyst
context: A maintenance and service-call module built as the first Smart Campus launch module, designed around the real operational needs of maintenance departments in youth villages and educational institutions.
goal_metrics: First Module to Launch, 5–30 Staff per Maintenance Department, End-to-End Workflow Coverage, 1000%+ Faster Issue Treatment Initiation, MVP → Full Redesign After 1 Year, and From Service Call to Inventory Counts.
discovery: Maintenance departments were working through paperwork, delays, and fragmented operational routines, creating a clear need for one digital workflow tailored to the education sector.
options_tradeoffs: The product direction prioritized a full departmental workflow over a narrow “open request” tool, and later used real-world MVP learning to justify a full redesign one year after launch.
execution: Defined workflows, bottlenecks, statuses, permissions, and operational logic for a module that connected service calls, priorities, tasks, equipment, and inventory as one end-to-end system.
results: The module created one digital maintenance workflow, dramatically shortened the time between issue discovery and treatment initiation, and established a foundational Smart Campus product module.
learnings: Strong process analysis, bottleneck identification, and tailored system definition can turn a daily operational pain point into the foundation of a real product.
